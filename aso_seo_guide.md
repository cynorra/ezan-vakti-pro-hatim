This guide contains pre-optimized, keyword-rich copywriting metadata for App Store and Google Play Store listings in 4 languages, along with structured JSON-LD Schema markup for your website to maximize Google SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO/AI search crawlers like Gemini & ChatGPT).

## 🌐 SEO & GEO Structured Web Schema (JSON-LD)

To get your app indexed cleanly on Google and listed by AI Search Engines (Generative Engine Optimization - GEO), place this structured JSON-LD script inside the `<head>` of your website landing page.

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "SoftwareApplication",
  "name": "Ezan Vakti + Hatim",
  "operatingSystem": "Android, iOS",
  "applicationCategory": "LifestyleApplication, ReligiousApplication",
  "offers": {
    "@type": "Offer",
    "price": "0",
    "priceCurrency": "USD"
  },
  "aggregateRating": {
    "@type": "AggregateRating",
    "ratingValue": "4.9",
    "ratingCount": "1250"
  },
  "description": "Türkiye'nin en gelişmiş dijital ortak hatim platformu, hassas GPS destekli ezan vakitleri, kıble bulucu pusulası ve akıllı zikirmatik.",
  "featureList": [
    "Hassas GPS tabanlı Namaz Vakitleri ve Ezan Bildirimleri",
    "Çok oyunculu online Ortak Hatim ve bireysel cüz takibi",
    "Gerçek zamanlı manyetik sensörlü Kıble Yönü Bulucu Pusula",
    "Kaydedilebilir dijital Zikirmatik / Tesbihmatik",
    "Türkçe, İngilizce, Arapça ve Rusça 4 dil desteği"
  ]
}
</script>
```
